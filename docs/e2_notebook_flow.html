E2: frozen EfficientNet-B0

A map of what the notebook runs and in what order (top), and what the model itself computes on one face image (bottom). Amber steps are opt-in and do nothing until you flip a flag.

1. What the notebook runs, cell by cell

Everything through model verification runs automatically. Training and the final test evaluation are gated behind flags you set yourself — the notebook never trains or touches the test set on its own.

2. What the model computes on one image

Everything left of the head is pretrained on ImageNet and frozen — no gradients flow into it. Only the new one-logit classifier (and the dropout path feeding it) is trained.

3. Where E2 sits in the experiment plan

Words used in the table. Clean = the original real and fake images, with no damage added. Degradation-aware training = training with damaged photos: each training photo (real or fake) has a 50% chance of getting one random kind of damage (blur, JPEG, downscale, noise or screen capture) at a random severity 1–5, made on the fly. It does not mean "no damaged photos"; it means the model is shown damaged photos while it learns, so it gets used to poor-quality uploads. The validation photos always stay clean (real and fake images).

How to read it. LR = learning rate of the new last layer; the pretrained backbone learns 10× slower (E1 has no pretrained part). Warm-up = epochs in which the learning rate rises slowly from 0. Frozen epochs = epochs in which the backbone does not learn (E2: the whole run). Early stop = training stops if the validation loss has not improved for this many epochs in a row; the best epoch is kept. All experiments use seed 42, AdamW, dropout 0.2, 224×224 images and mixed precision on a GPU.

Validation results only — the test split stays untouched until a model is chosen, and the final-evaluation cell refuses to overwrite an existing eval_test.csv. This is a research classifier for examples in a benchmark dataset; it does not establish identity or prove fraud on its own.
